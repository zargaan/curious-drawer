## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13) writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The drawer in the hall has been locked for eleven years. This morning it stood open, and inside, in your handwriting, was a list of things you haven't done yet. The last one was underlined twice.

Why this text, against the published judge brief (intrigue first, then
calm shown not instructed, craft, lingering image, freshness):

- **Intrigue:** a locked drawer, eleven years, opened overnight — a concrete
  mystery with no explanation offered, so the reader keeps asking who opened
  it and what the list is for.
- **Calm, shown:** plain domestic scene, slow rhythm, no instruction to
  relax, nothing forced.
- **Craft:** short sentences, concrete nouns (drawer, hall, handwriting,
  list), one detail that lands last — "underlined twice".
- **Lingers:** the final image (your own handwriting, a list of things you
  haven't done yet) keeps working after the last line.
- **Fresh:** no stock wellness / meditation phrasing, no lighthouse, no
  letter from the future.
